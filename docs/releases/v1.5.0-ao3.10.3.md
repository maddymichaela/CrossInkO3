# CrossInk AO3.10.3

AO3.10.3 was an internal compatibility and validation milestone while updating this fork to the newer CrossInk base. It was not tagged, published, or distributed as firmware.

The completed work was rolled directly into `v1.5.1-ao3.11`, avoiding an extra firmware version that would immediately be obsolete. There is therefore no `ao3.10.3` binary to flash. Use `ao3.10.2` to remain on the CrossInk 1.5.0 base, or `ao3.11` for the completed CrossInk 1.5.1 integration.
